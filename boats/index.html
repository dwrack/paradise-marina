In the water

Slips in the basin

The basin sits behind the old casino building, tucked in off the bayou. It is a working harbor, so your neighbors are as likely to be shrimp boats as bay boats.

Slips are rented month to month on a written agreement. Send us the boat's length, beam and draft and we will match you to a slip.

The dry yard

A wide gravel lot between the basin and the bayou. Park a boat on its trailer, an RV, a work trailer or equipment.

Anything stored in the yard has to be registered, insured and able to roll. That matters most in hurricane season, when you may need to move it on short notice.

Good for
Boats on trailers, RVs, utility and equipment trailers
