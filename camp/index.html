The idea

Your own trailer, kayak and e-bike

We are planning a small camp on the north side of the basin: a short row of Airstreams facing the water, each with a deck, a fire pit, and its own kayak and e-bike.

The kayak launch is already here. New Orleans Kayak Swamp Tours runs a self-guided paddle from this dock into Bayou Bienvenue, where the herons and the gators are.

Where this stands: the camp is in planning and still needs its permits. We have not set an opening date or prices, and we are not taking reservations. The list below is only so we can tell you when that changes.
