The job

What you would do

Mowing and weed control, trash, gates and locks, shed doors and roofs, lighting, pressure washing, dock upkeep, storm prep, and letting vendors in. Jack of all trades, strong in a few.

The deal
Your RV site with power and water, in exchange for a set amount of work each month, done on your own schedule. Extra work is paid hourly.
Big plus
Carpentry, basic electrical and plumbing, small engines. Welding is a bonus.
Room to grow
We are planning a few glamping sites on the water. The right person could help build them and then run them, with pay to match.
