Who rents here

Built for people who work out of a truck

Most of our tenants are in the trades: roofers, mechanics, sheet metal, boat people. They want a dry place close to the city to keep tools, materials and a trailer, and they want to pull right up to the door.

If you need more than storage, ask. Some units are set up as small workshops, with light shop work written into the lease.

Louisiana storage rule worth knowing: what you keep in a unit is yours to insure. We do not insure tenant property, so carry your own coverage on anything valuable.

What you get
An enclosed shed with an overhead door. You bring the lock, and you hold the only key.
Rent
From $300 a month, depending on the unit. Due on the 1st.
Term
Month to month, with 30 days notice either way. Longer terms on request.
Deposit
One month's rent.
Power
Not standard. We can run power to some units and bill it at cost.
Workshop use
Light work is allowed in some units with our written OK. No vehicle repair shops, no hazardous materials.
How to pay
Zelle, Venmo or cash with a receipt.

How it works

From first call to keys, usually the same week

  1. Tell us what you need to store. Tools, a trailer, materials, a small shop. We will tell you which units are open.

  2. Come look. We walk the unit with you so you see the door, the floor and the access before you commit.

  3. Sign online. The lease comes to your phone. First month and deposit are due at signing.

  4. Put your lock on it. Move in.
