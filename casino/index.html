The old casino

The big blue building out front, with the script sign on the roof. It has sat quiet for a while. It is coming back as the place to eat, drink and gather on this stretch of the bayou.

The building

A roadside landmark with its back to the water

From Paris Road you see the deco entrance. Walk through and the far wall is glass, looking straight onto the basin and the boats.

Inside there is a commercial kitchen, a long bar, and one big open hall. The old gaming floor still has its chandeliers and mirrored ceiling.

What is next

Food, drinks and a room for a party
